Nonlinear System with State-Dependent Representation: From Model-Based to Direct Data-Driven Controller

Lidong Li, Rui Huang, Lin Zhao†
National University of Singapore
Preprint paper - coming soon (arXiv) Code and data

Abstract

This paper proposes a direct data-driven controller design approach for nonlinear systems based on their state dependent representation. We regard this representation as a form of state-dependent parameter-varying model, which allows us to derive new model-based conditions for nonlinear system control. The closed-loop properties are analyzed in terms of the robustness against disturbances and the region of attraction under input saturation. The model-based conditions are then adapted to the data-driven case, where a library of known basis functions is assumed, and the state-dependent representation takes the form of a linear combination of them, with unknown coefficients. Noisy experimental data introduces uncertainties, and our proposed direct data-driven controller ensures local stability for all nonlinear systems whose coefficients are consistent with the data. Based solely on data, we rigorously characterize the closed-loop system's robustness against disturbances and its region of attraction under input saturation. The theoretical results are validated through numerical examples and physical experiment.
